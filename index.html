About DataSense

Built by analysts, for teams tired of manual data firefighting.

We are three data science students at NED University of Engineering and Technology, Karachi. Working on analytics projects, we noticed that data analysts spend up to 80% of their time manually cleaning spreadsheets, fixing typos, and resolving duplicate entries before doing any real work.

Existing tools are either over-simplified spreadsheet macros or massive enterprise software requiring dedicated data engineering teams. Moreover, almost zero existing platforms natively understand mixed Urdu and English data—a pervasive issue in South Asian enterprises and local organisations. We built DataSense as the tool we wished we had.

The Unspoken Data Crisis

Bad data enters silently.
It fails loudly at report time.

What happens & Where

Inconsistent entries like Karachi, karachi, KHI, and کراچی sneak into systems during manual data entry, system exports, or spreadsheet consolidation.

Why it matters

Decisions are made on aggregated numbers. When regional entries fail to combine due to typos or script differences, executive dashboards skew, inventory counts drift, and reports lose stakeholder trust.

How teams cope today (and why it breaks)

Teams rely on manual spreadsheet filtering, fragile Python scripts, or enterprise tools that require specialized data engineering teams. These approaches are slow, lack repair rationale, and completely ignore mixed-script Urdu text.

Why Data Science is Needed

Static regex rules cannot catch context-dependent mistakes or phonetic Urdu/English duplicates (e.g. matching "Karachi" with "کراچی"). Machine learning models learn normal distribution patterns per column, vectorizing text to calculate semantic proximity and flag true anomalies.

Key Differentiator

"Fix with Proof" Philosophy

Unlike black-box automated tools that silently overwrite database records, DataSense provides an impact preview, a confidence score, and clear reasons for every single proposed fix. Nothing changes in your underlying database without explicit human approval.
